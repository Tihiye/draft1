INTJ

The Architect

Strategic, independent, and imaginative. Enjoys creating plans and solving complex problems.

INTP

The Logician

Curious, analytical, and inventive. Loves exploring ideas and understanding how things work.

ENTJ

The Commander

Confident, decisive, and ambitious. Naturally takes charge and enjoys achieving goals.

ENTP

The Debater

Energetic, clever, and curious. Enjoys challenging ideas and discovering new possibilities.

INFJ

The Advocate

Thoughtful, insightful, and idealistic. Wants to help others and create meaningful change.

INFP

The Mediator

Creative, empathetic, and idealistic. Values authenticity and meaningful relationships.

ENFJ

The Protagonist

Charismatic, caring, and inspiring. Enjoys helping people reach their potential.

ENFP

The Campaigner

Enthusiastic, creative, and sociable. Loves exploring possibilities and connecting with people.

ISTJ

The Logistician

Responsible, organized, and dependable. Values accuracy, structure, and tradition.

ISFJ

The Defender

Loyal, caring, and hardworking. Enjoys supporting and protecting the people around them.

ESTJ

The Executive

Organized, practical, and direct. Good at managing people, projects, and responsibilities.

ESFJ

The Consul

Friendly, caring, and social. Enjoys creating harmony and
helping others.

ISTP

The Virtuoso

Practical, curious, and adventurous. Enjoys experimenting and solving hands-on problems.

ISFP

The Adventurer

Artistic, sensitive, and spontaneous. Appreciates beauty, freedom, and new experiences.

ESTP

The Entrepreneur

Energetic, bold, and action-oriented. Enjoys taking risks and living in the moment.

ESFP

The Entertainer

Fun-loving, energetic, and spontaneous. Enjoys entertaining people and experiencing life.
